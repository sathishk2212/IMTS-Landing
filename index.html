Where Tezo fits

We automate the work between the systems.

Not a generic “AI for manufacturing” pitch. We look for the point where a digital workflow quietly becomes a human workflow - and where exceptions, handoffs and fragmented data make scale expensive.

Accelerator 01 · Hero
RFQ emailCatalogue + ERPMargin checkQuote

Order Entry & RFQ Automation

A customer RFQ arrives the way customer RFQs actually arrive: an email, a spreadsheet, a drawing attached. The agent reads it, matches every line to your catalogue and your ERP, checks stock and lead time, prices it against your margin floor and drafts the quote. A person reads that draft and sends it.

Why it's differentThe margin floor lives in the interface, not in a policy document. A quote that breaks it cannot be sent. Going below it is an exception with a name on it, a reason attached and a record left behind. And it doesn't stop at the quote: the order, the invoice and the cash application run on the same thread.
What you'll see: an RFQ email land, and a priced quote come back out of it.
Accelerator 02
ERPWMSSupplierAsk

Supply Chain Ops Copilot

Orders, inventory, supplier and shipment data pulled into one conversational layer, so teams can ask what changed, why it matters and what needs attention right now instead of hunting across four systems for the same answer.

Why it's differentIt isn't another dashboard. It turns fragmented operational data into a decision and follow-through workflow, with the reasoning behind every answer shown alongside it.
What you'll see: a supply change land, and the copilot flag who needs to know and why.
Accelerator 03
DemandInventoryAction

Demand, Inventory & Supply Intelligence

Demand signals, inventory positions, supply constraints and historical patterns combined to surface risk early, so planners spend their attention on the decisions that actually need judgment.

Why it's differentForecasting becomes a decision workflow, not another forecast file sitting next to the ERP export.
What you'll see: a stock-out risk surfaced days before it would have shown up on a report.

Service & Dispatch Intelligence

Everything that moves after the product leaves the factory — technicians, appointments, parts, routes, capacity and service commitments — optimised against the SLA, not just the nearest open slot.
